About ShopEase

ShopEase is a modern e-commerce storefront built with HTML, Tailwind CSS, and Vanilla JavaScript. We offer a wide range of products across multiple categories, from electronics to jewelry and clothing.

Our mission is to provide a seamless shopping experience with quality products at great prices. This is a demonstration project using the Fake Store API for product data.

Our Values

  • Customer-first approach
  • Quality products at fair prices
  • Transparent and secure checkout
  • Fast and reliable delivery
